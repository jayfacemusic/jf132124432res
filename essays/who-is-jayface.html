Who Is Jayface?

Jayface • Published: 2026. October 21.

A Producer, DJ, and Creative World Built Over More Than Two Decades

Jayface is the artist name of Akos Juhasz, an electronic music producer and DJ whose musical identity is rooted primarily in trance while extending into other areas of electronic music. Over more than two decades, Jayface has developed beyond a recording alias into a broader creative identity encompassing music, visual design, label work, mix show, and writing. These activities are interconnected expressions of a shared creative vision.

Jayface created the name in 2005 as a deliberate part of this identity. The associated logo and visual world were also developed by Jayface to embody the same vision, becoming integral to how the artist is represented and recognized. Together, they established a distinctive creative signature connecting the music with its visual presentation.

Musically, Jayface has never been defined by a single, rigid genre. Trance remains at the heart of the sound, alongside uplifting, melodic, and progressive trance, psytrance, raw trance, melodic techno, and earlier explorations of minimal house. These different directions reflect an ongoing interest in exploring electronic music while maintaining a recognizable artistic character.

The production process extends beyond composition alone. Jayface develops each track through every stage of production, from its initial composition to its final mix and master, shaping its sound, atmosphere, and structure throughout the process.

This musical vision also extends to Jayface Music, the label founded and directed by Jayface, and Jayface Radio, the mix show he also curates and hosts. The label provides a framework for releasing music, while the radio series reflects his musical taste through a curated selection of trance and melodic techno, mixing together tracks by other artists and his own productions into a distinctive musical journey.

Visual creation plays an equally important role in the presentation of the music. Jayface's visual work includes cover artwork, graphic design, photography, and branding, all of which establish the atmosphere and give individual releases their own visual identity. Rather than simply illustrating a sound, these elements form a visual language consistent with the music itself.

Writing offers another way to explore ideas and perspectives. Alongside music, Jayface writes essays on culture, technology, aesthetics, society, nature, and philosophy. Although these subjects extend beyond music production, they share an interest in the ideas, patterns, and underlying forces that shape how people experience the world.

The official website, jayface.com (also entirely designed and coded by Jayface), brings these activities together, providing a central place for music, releases, visual work, mixes, and essays. It reflects the breadth of the project while allowing each area to retain its own identity.

Today, Jayface represents an evolving creative world built over more than two decades. Music remains its foundation, while visual creation, label work, a curated mix show, and writing extend its reach across different forms of expression. What connects them is a consistent creative perspective that continues to shape the identity of Jayface.

For a more personal account of Jayface’s musical journey and the story behind the music, read The Birth of Melodies.
