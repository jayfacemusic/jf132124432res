The Birth of Melodies

Jayface • Published: 2026. September 8.

An Artist Memoir

Looking back at more than two decades of making music, it is difficult to separate the music itself from the many stages, changes, pauses, and unexpected turns that shaped the journey behind it. What began with classic tracker programs eventually grew into a long-term creative identity, followed by years away from production and, later, an entirely unexpected return. This is a condensed account of that journey - from the earliest experiments and the birth of Jayface to the releases, projects, and milestones that have shaped my music over the years.

The Beginnings: When Music Ran Vertically

My musical journey began in the early 2000s, working with what are now considered classic, top-down vertical sequencers (tracker programs). That was where I learned the raw fundamentals of electronic music production. The turning point came in late 2003 when, as a member of a trio formation, I stepped into a professional studio for the very first time. It was a massive milestone - we were working on a large-scale stage production enhanced by a live vocalist. Even though this material was never commercially released, holding that physical master CD in our hands meant everything at the time. That was the moment music shifted from a hobby into a true calling.

Around 2004, a massive shift occurred in my production workflow as I transitioned from vertical trackers to modern, left-to-right Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs). Not long after, in 2005, my musical identity was born: I officially began writing and registering music under the alias Jayface.

The First Album

The year 2006 was all about refining my signature sound. This period birthed ‘Midnight’ and ‘Awakening’ - two tracks carrying deep, late-night energies. These two songs laid the foundation for my very first official release, the ‘Eye EP’, which came out in 2007. That same year, I launched the jayface.com domain to create a permanent digital home for both my music and my graphic design work.

The years that followed were fueled by pure creativity: 2008 - ‘Perfection EP’: A conceptual release inspired by the perfection of nature. It consisted of three distinct yet interconnected tracks (A, B, and C) reflecting my inner world. 2009 - ‘Summer Trance Dreams 2009’: My first full-length, 15-track artist album. It represented countless hours of studio work, bridging the gap between uplifting trance and deep minimal house across a spectrum ranging from 95 to 140 BPM. Following this chapter, I made the decision to step away from music production for a long time.

The Return (2020)

After a long hiatus, I returned to the studio in 2020. Something new sparked inside me, and that energy materialized into my track ‘Let Go’, a song about release and the strength of moving forward. The track was signed to the prestigious Pure Trance label by an absolute legend of the genre and my personal favorite producer, Solarstone.

‘Let Go’ had an incredible run: Solarstone played it as an ID in his sets for months, and it was featured as the “Big Tune” on Episode 240 of Pure Trance Radio, later making it onto the prestigious ‘Pure Trance NEON: Retrospective 2020’ compilation at the end of the year. Another legend, Johan Gielen, selected it among his monthly favorites and sent fantastic professional feedback. It received heavy support from acts like the duo Stoneface & Terminal. On the iTunes charts, it climbed all the way to No. 1 on the Trance chart in Mexico, reached the Top 10 in the USA and Switzerland, and was featured among Beatport’s top new releases.

In 2021, I responded to the confinement of lockdowns with the uplifting track ‘Escape’ (which Johan Gielen again selected for his monthly favorites, and it broke into the Beatport Trance Top 100). This was followed by the carefree, summer-vibe track ‘Fly Away’, which climbed to No. 48 on Beatport, and the more experimental, psy-trance-infused ‘Gravity’, which marched to No. 12 on the Juno Download Hard Trance chart.

In November 2021, I made a definitive choice: I decided to release all my future music exclusively through my own independent artist label. Following an immense amount of work, December 2022 saw the release of my studio album, sleekly titled ‘The Album’. Across 20 tracks, it showcased my entire musical spectrum, from progressive sounds to pure, uplifting trance.

Mix Series, Albums, and Milestones

Jayface Radio (2023): I launched my own one-hour monthly mix series on YouTube, focusing on the worlds of trance and melodic techno. ‘Meet Her At The Club’ Album (2023): The fruit of a full year of studio dedication, pushing the boundaries between tech house and trance. ‘The Tree Of Life’ (2024–2025): A two-part, deep, nature-inspired musical journey. Both the first part (‘Beginnings’) and the sequel (‘Infinity’) earned spots in the Beatport Essentials selections. ‘Drifting Worlds’ Album (2025): I reached my next milestone: another comprehensive album representing the trance genre as a whole, moving between 126 and 138 BPM. ‘Blue’ (2025): I closed out 2025 with this emotional, uplifting track, which climbed to No. 26 on the Beatport Trance chart.

The Present

I recently celebrated the 50th episode of Jayface Radio, which marked a major milestone for me. My latest release at the time was ‘A Moment In Space’, a highly energetic, 140 BPM fusion of driving trance and hypnotic psytrance, broken up in the middle by a dark, deep ambient breakdown. Since then, the journey has continued with ‘Sphere’ , a dark and hypnotic 125 BPM track that continues my exploration of the space between techno and raw trance, driven by a brooding club groove and a deep, mysterious breakdown.

The story is still unfolding. Looking back, the path from those early experiments with tracker programs to where I am today was anything but linear. There were years of intense creative activity, years of silence, changes in direction, and entirely new chapters that could not have been anticipated at the beginning. What has remained constant throughout all of it is the desire to keep creating, exploring, and seeing where the music can go next.
