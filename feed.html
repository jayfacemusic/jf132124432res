'Let Go' is Out Now!

After a long hiatus, I finally returned to the studio to create 'Let Go' - a track about new beginnings and the power of moving forward. It is a massive honor to have this record released on Solarstone’s legendary label. Richard’s support means a lot to me, especially his words: 'This fabulous uplifter was an ID in Solarstone's sets for months, but is finally yours to have!' The track was featured in episodes 240 and 241 of Pure Trance Radio, earning the coveted 'Big Tune' title in episode 240. I am so grateful to him for giving me this fresh start. The reception has been incredible. On the iTunes Charts, 'Let Go' climbed to No. 1 on the Trance Chart in Mexico, No. 5 on the Dance Chart in India, No. 8 in the United States, No. 19 in India (Trance), and No. 31 in Spain (Trance). On Beatport, it was featured in the Closing Essentials and Latest Releases for Trance, peaking at No. 11 and gaining widespread playlist support. Besides Solarstone, 'Let Go' was included in the monthly selection by the iconic Johan Gielen - and I am also incredibly thankful for his kind words. Additionally, it has been supported by the powerhouse duo Stoneface & Terminal, and featured on TranceFamily. A huge thanks to all the DJs who supported the track, and to every listener worldwide who made this comeback unforgettable.

'Let Go'

2004. December 21.

The shift

Around 2004, a massive shift occurred in my production workflow as I transitioned from vertical trackers to modern, left-to-right Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs). This new layout opened up a completely different way of arranging and visualizing sound. Armed with this fresh perspective, I spent the following months heavily involved behind the scenes, participating in several side projects as a co-composer and contributing to various underground electronic tracks.

2003. December 19.

The beginnings

In the early 2000s, my musical journey began with vertical, top-down sequencers, learning the raw grit of electronic music production. By late 2003, this experimentation led to a major milestone: entering a professional studio as part of a trio collaboration. Together, we crafted and engineered our very first official master CD, conceived as a full-scale stage production featuring a live vocalist. While this material never received a commercial release, that physical disc remains a foundational piece of my history - the moment the music moved from a hobby to a serious craft.
